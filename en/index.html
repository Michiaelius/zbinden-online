FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a GEO audit?

A GEO audit (Generative Engine Optimization) checks whether AI search systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI find a business, understand it correctly and recommend it. Zbinden Software assesses six weighted criteria for this – AI citability, brand authority, E-E-A-T, technical GEO, structured data and platform optimization – and delivers a prioritized action plan.

How does GEO differ from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO targets rankings in Google's list of links. GEO aims for a business to be named as a source and recommended within the answers of AI systems. The technical basis overlaps, but GEO puts additional weight on citable content, structured data and clear signals about company identity.

Which AI systems are checked in the GEO audit?

The currently most-used AI answer systems are checked: ChatGPT including web search, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews and Gemini.

Which businesses benefit from process automation?

SMEs with recurring, manual tasks that noticeably cost time – duplicate data entry, scattered files or paper-based approvals. The focus is on solutions that are quick to deploy with measurable benefit, not months-long IT projects.
