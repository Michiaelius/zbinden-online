FAQ

Frequently asked questions

May my employees enter customer data into ChatGPT?

Only with great care. The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection also applies to AI tools. In the workshop, your team learns which data is off-limits and which settings and versions are safer.

Does my team need any prior knowledge?

No. The workshop is for all employees, including those who have never used AI.

Does the workshop take place at our premises?

Yes, the half-day and full-day formats take place on site at your company. The introduction is also available online.

What happens after the workshop?

You receive a list of tasks that can be automated. If you wish, I implement these automations.

How do I book a workshop?

Through a free 30-minute intro call. We clarify your industry, the size of your team and the right format.
