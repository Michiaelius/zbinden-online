What a Quick Win looks like in practice

Illustrative scenario based on common SME processes — not a real client reference, but a calculation you can check.

Example: quote preparation in inside sales
  • Starting point: quotes are assembled by hand — look up line items in the ERP, check prices, fill the template, generate the PDF. Around 40 minutes per quote, about 15 quotes a week.
  • Implementation: an agent pulls the line items from the ERP, calculates prices from defined rules, fills the quote template and files the PDF. The employee reviews and sends.
  • Result: from about 40 to 6 minutes per quote. Roughly 8 hours a week recovered.

    How is this share estimated?

    The slider positions itself automatically based on the type of task you pick. It's our estimate, not a measurement: adjust it freely to your reality.

    The principle: count what is genuinely mechanical — looking up information, copy-paste, filling templates, formatting — and set aside what needs human judgement or handling special cases. You also need to keep some time to check the result.

    • 70–90% — highly repetitive, well-defined task: data entry, data extraction, follow-ups, document generation.
    • 40–60% — mixed task: a mechanical part, a few decisions case by case.
    • 15–30% — task that mostly relies on analysis, advice or the client relationship.

    Example. Preparing a quote takes ~40 min: look up line items in the ERP, check prices, fill the template, generate the PDF. The agent prepares everything, the employee reviews and sends in ~6 min — about 85% less time on this task.
