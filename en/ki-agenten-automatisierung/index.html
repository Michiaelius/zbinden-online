    Data protection

    Your data stays with you

    Automation does not mean your data leaves the building. Before every project, I agree with you in writing which data flows where.

    Runs on your accounts

    The automation works in your existing environment, for example in Microsoft 365 or in your business applications. I do not store any client data on my side.

    Data location agreed in writing

    The default is processing in Switzerland or in the EU. For particularly sensitive data, I choose solutions hosted in Switzerland. As an option, the AI model can also run locally on a computer in your company; the data then doesn't leave your network.

    Sensitive data processed separately

    For delicate content, identifying details such as names, addresses or customer numbers can be replaced with placeholders before AI processing. The AI service then sees only the content, not the person; the mapping stays in your environment and is only restored there. Whether that is sufficient for your process is clarified case by case.

    No training on your data

    I only use AI services that are contractually bound not to use your data to train their models.

    Data processing agreement

    With every client I sign an agreement under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP). It governs access, confidentiality and deletion.

    What does the CHF 900 fixed price for Standard Process Automation include?

A typical example is networking automation: you photograph a business card and add a short note, the contact is saved automatically in your CRM or a spreadsheet, and a draft follow-up email is ready for review.     When is a standard process enough, and when is a Process Check needed?

    A standard process is enough when the process is clearly scoped and connects to a destination with a standard interface, such as a common CRM or a spreadsheet. If it needs adjustments to your specific processes, several processes, or a connection to existing systems such as an ERP or industry software, the next step is the Process Check at a fixed price of CHF 2'200. Its price is fully credited if implementation follows. We work out which case applies in the free intro call.

    What happens in the free intro call?

You get an assessment of whether a standard process for CHF 900 is enough, whether a Process Check is worth it, or whether automation doesn't pay off at all. The call is free and without obligation; you book the time directly online.

    Is Standard Process Automation the same as GEO optimization for CHF 900?
