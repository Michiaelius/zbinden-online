FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

A one-pager — a single page with everything that matters — starts at CHF 1'800, a multi-page website at CHF 4'800. I'll give you the exact price after the free initial call — depending on how many pages and features you need.

How long does it take?

A one-pager is usually ready in about 2 weeks, a multi-page website in 3–4 weeks — depending on how quickly information and images are available.

Where will the website be hosted?

Whatever is easiest for you: if you already have a domain and hosting, I replace the website right there. Otherwise, I set up new hosting. Ongoing domain and hosting costs are not included in the price — with the care plan, hosting is included. The domain stays registered in your name — it belongs to you.

Who writes the texts?

I do. You give me the information — in a conversation or in writing — and I turn it into texts that match the design and make people want to get in touch. You review everything, and of course you can have anything changed later.

Who provides the photos?

You provide the photos — that way, the image rights are clearly yours. If you like, I can take photos myself or organize a photographer; these costs are billed separately.

What if something changes later?

With the care plan at CHF 79 per month, I handle small changes such as texts, images, opening hours and news (up to about 1 hour per month) for you — hosting included. Minimum term 6 months, then cancellable monthly. Without the plan, changes are billed by time spent at CHF 120 per hour.

What does “GEO-ready” mean?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: the website is built so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI understand who you are, what you offer and where to find you — through structured data, clean HTML and open access for AI crawlers.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not necessarily. The free AI check shows whether ChatGPT and Perplexity mention you today. Often, targeted changes to your existing website are enough — that's what the GEO optimization is for, from CHF 900. A new website is only worth it when the current one no longer works for your visitors: outdated, hard to read on a phone or missing the content it needs. If the cause lies outside the website, the GEO audit will show it.

Is a GEO audit credited?

Yes — if you commission a new multi-page website from me after a GEO audit, the audit price is fully credited.
