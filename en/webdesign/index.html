FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

A one-pager — a single page with everything that matters — costs CHF 1'790, a website with about 6 pages CHF 3'900. By subscription you pay instead CHF 490 setup and CHF 119 per month (one-pager) or CHF 990 setup and CHF 199 per month (multiple pages), maintenance and changes of up to 30 min or 1 hr per month included. Additional pages or languages are billed by time spent; legal notice and privacy policy don't count as pages. I give you the exact price after the free initial call.

Buy or subscribe — what's the difference?

The service is the same, only the way you pay differs. When you buy, you pay for the website once and add maintenance if you want. With a subscription, you pay a setup fee at the start and then a fixed monthly price from launch, maintenance included, plus changes of up to 30 min per month (one-pager) or 1 hr per month (multiple pages); unused time expires at the end of the month. Minimum term 24 months, then open-ended and cancellable monthly to the end of the month, with no renewal into a new minimum term. The price doesn't increase during the minimum term. You can take over the website at any time: purchase price minus the setup. Domain, content and data always belong to you, with a free export at the end.

How long does it take?

Expect about 2 weeks for a one-pager and 3–4 weeks for a multi-page website — from receipt of all materials, plus time for your feedback.

Where will the website be hosted?

Whatever is easiest for you: if you already have a domain and hosting, I replace the website right there. Otherwise I set up new hosting. Domain fees are not included in the price. If I host the website, hosting is included in the maintenance or the subscription. The domain stays registered in your name — it belongs to you.

Who writes the texts?

I do. You give me the information — in a conversation or in writing — and I turn it into texts that match the design and make people want to get in touch. You review everything, and of course you can have anything changed later.

Who provides the photos?

You provide the photos — that way, the image rights are clearly yours. If you like, I can take photos myself or organize a photographer; these costs are billed separately.

What if something changes later?

Just tell me what needs changing. With a subscription, changes of up to 30 min per month (one-pager) or 1 hr per month (multiple pages) are included; unused time expires at the end of the month. Beyond that, and when you buy, changes are billed by time spent: CHF 120 per hour, minimum charge 30 minutes per request, then per started 15 minutes. A small change such as a new phone number therefore costs CHF 60.

How much does hosting my website cost?

Hosting, SSL certificate, monitoring and technical updates are part of the maintenance: from CHF 39 per month for a one-pager, from CHF 69 per month for a multi-page website. With a subscription, maintenance is included. If you already have your own hosting, I upload the website there directly. The domain stays in your name. If you end our collaboration, you receive your website's files.

What does “GEO-ready” mean?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: the website is built so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI understand who you are, what you offer and where to find you — through structured data, clean HTML and open access for AI crawlers.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not necessarily. The free AI check shows whether ChatGPT and Perplexity mention you today. Often, targeted adjustments to your existing website are enough: the GEO audit (CHF 490–790) shows what's missing, the implementation costs CHF 990–1'490. A new website is only worth it if the current one no longer works for your visitors: outdated, hard to read on a smartphone or missing the content people need.
