FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

A one-pager — a single page with everything that matters — costs CHF 1'790, a website with about 6 pages CHF 3'900. By subscription you pay instead, with no setup fee, CHF 139 per month (one-pager) or CHF 239 per month (multiple pages); with a start fee of CHF 490 or CHF 990, only CHF 119 or CHF 199 per month, maintenance and changes of up to 30 min or 1 hr per month included. Additional pages or languages are billed by time spent; legal notice and privacy policy don't count as pages. I give you the exact price after the free initial call.

Buy or subscribe — what's the difference?

The service is the same, only the way you pay differs. When you buy, you pay for the website once and add maintenance if you want. With a subscription, there is no setup fee, just a fixed monthly price from launch (lower if you choose to pay a start fee), maintenance included, plus changes of up to 30 min per month (one-pager) or 1 hr per month (multiple pages); unused time expires at the end of the month. Minimum term 24 months, then open-ended and cancellable monthly to the end of the month, with no renewal into a new minimum term. The price doesn't increase during the minimum term. The website remains my property during the subscription. To keep it after the subscription, you buy it at the purchase price (minus any start fee paid); you can also do this at any time before. Without a purchase, the website is taken offline when the subscription ends. Domain, content and data always belong to you, with a free export at the end.

How long does it take?

Expect about 2 weeks for a one-pager and 3–4 weeks for a multi-page website — from receipt of all materials, plus time for your feedback.

Can my website be in German and French?

Yes. German and French are both my native languages, and I studied in a trinational programme across Switzerland, Germany and France. I write the second language version myself, no machine translation, and set it up so that Google and AI assistants like ChatGPT show the right language. That way you also reach customers in French- or German-speaking Switzerland, or across the Rhine in Baden and Alsace. An additional language is billed by time spent.

Where will the website be hosted?

In Switzerland: I host my clients' websites with Infomaniak, an independent Swiss provider running its own data centres in Switzerland on renewable electricity. Your data therefore stays in Switzerland. If I host the website, hosting is included in the maintenance or the subscription. If you already have a domain and hosting, I can also replace the website right there. Domain fees are not included in the price. The domain stays registered in your name — it belongs to you.

Who writes the texts?

I do. You give me the information — in a conversation or in writing — and I turn it into texts that match the design and make people want to get in touch. You review everything, and of course you can have anything changed later.

Who provides the photos?

You provide the photos — that way, the image rights are clearly yours. If you like, I can take photos myself or organize a photographer; these costs are billed separately.

What if something changes later?

Just tell me what needs changing. With a subscription, changes of up to 30 min per month (one-pager) or 1 hr per month (multiple pages) are included; unused time expires at the end of the month. Beyond that, and when you buy, changes are billed by time spent: CHF 120 per hour, minimum charge 30 minutes per request, then per started 15 minutes. A small change such as a new phone number therefore costs CHF 60.

How much does hosting my website cost?

Hosting, SSL certificate, monitoring, technical updates and small adjustments of up to 15 min per month (e.g. opening hours, phone number, prices) are part of the maintenance: from CHF 39 per month or CHF 390 per year for a one-pager, from CHF 69 per month or CHF 690 per year for a multi-page website. Larger changes are quoted separately (CHF 120 per hour). With a subscription, maintenance is included. If you already have your own hosting, I upload the website there directly. The domain stays in your name. If you end our collaboration, you receive your website's files.

What does “GEO-ready” mean?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: the website is built so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI understand who you are, what you offer and where to find you — through structured data, clean HTML and open access for AI crawlers.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not necessarily. The free AI check shows whether ChatGPT and Perplexity mention you today. Often, targeted adjustments to your existing website are enough: the GEO audit (CHF 490–790) shows what's missing, the implementation costs CHF 990–1'490. A new website is only worth it if the current one no longer works for your visitors: outdated, hard to read on a smartphone or missing the content people need.
