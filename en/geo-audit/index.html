What I typically find

Patterns that repeat

Common

No Organization or industry-specific schema (e.g. LegalService, Dentist, LocalBusiness) — search engines and AI systems can't reliably match name, address and opening hours.

Common

No legal notice (Impressum), or one linking to a page that doesn't exist — a trust issue that goes beyond GEO.

Regular

Structured data with the wrong contact email — usually a leftover from the website build that nobody fixed.

Regular

No named, qualified person of responsibility — whether physician, lawyer or master craftsperson: one of the most important trust signals, often missing.

Anonymized, recurring patterns from past audits — no details about individual companies.

About me
