Web app · Personal project

Kinderbörse

Exchange for children’s clothes and toys · Fricktal

A WhatsApp group of more than 500 members swaps children’s clothes and toys — but in the chat, every offer is buried within hours. Kinderbörse gives the listings a permanent home: search, filter, find again. Contact still happens through WhatsApp.

  • Search by category, size, age, price and radius around your own postcode
  • Child profiles: one tap shows what fits your child right now
  • Built for phones — members join through an invitation link, no app store needed
  • Three days from the first idea to the launch in the group

AI tool · Built in-house

AI visibility test

Does ChatGPT mention your company?

More and more people ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for a recommendation instead of googling. This tool asks both systems eight typical customer questions about an industry and a place, live — and records whether a company is named in the answers.

  • Eight questions, the way real customers ask them — live to ChatGPT and Perplexity
  • The result is a short PDF report in plain language, without jargon
  • The basis of my free AI check and of the GEO audit

Background

As a Junior Consultant at Drees & Sommer Switzerland, my focus was then the interface between business units, clients and development: analysing requirements, structuring them and translating them into workable technical solutions.
